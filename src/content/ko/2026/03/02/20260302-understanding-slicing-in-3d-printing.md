## Understanding Slicing in 3D Printing
![image](https://cdn.creality.com/ow/official/92d86936-853b-48b0-ba6b-08581d027a28.png)

Slicing is a crucial process in 3D printing that converts 3D models into instructions for printers. It involves breaking down the model into layers, determining the path for the printer's nozzle, and setting parameters like speed and temperature. Proper slicing ensures optimal print quality and efficiency.
