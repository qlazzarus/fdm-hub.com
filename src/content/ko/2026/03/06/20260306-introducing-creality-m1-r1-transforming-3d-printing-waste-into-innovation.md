## Introducing Creality M1 & R1: Turn 3D Printing Waste Into Creative Power!
![image](https://cdn.creality.com/ow/official/8cbde2f5-cbae-4106-8f83-58c8b922e1b1.jpg)

Creality has launched the M1 and R1 models, designed to recycle 3D printing waste into usable filament. These machines aim to reduce material waste and promote sustainability in 3D printing by allowing users to repurpose failed prints and leftover materials into new creations.
