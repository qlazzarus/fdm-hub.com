## Bambu Lab and meland Launch China's First 3D Printing Creativity Center for Children
![image](https://blog.bambulab.com/content/images/2026/03/BBLxmeland---hero.jpg)

Bambu Lab and meland have opened a pioneering 3D Printing Creativity Center in China aimed at inspiring children to explore and engage with 3D printing technology. The center offers hands-on workshops and educational programs designed to foster creativity and innovation among young learners, providing them with the tools and knowledge to create their own 3D printed projects.
