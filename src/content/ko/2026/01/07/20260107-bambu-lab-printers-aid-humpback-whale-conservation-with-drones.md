## Bambu Lab Printers Aid Humpback Whale Conservation with Modern Drones
![image](https://blog.bambulab.com/content/images/2026/01/Whaser-Project--4-.jpg)

The WHASER Project utilizes advanced drones to monitor and conserve humpback whales, with Bambu Lab printers providing essential components for drone construction. These 3D printed parts enhance the drones' functionality and efficiency, showcasing the integration of 3D printing technology in wildlife conservation efforts.
