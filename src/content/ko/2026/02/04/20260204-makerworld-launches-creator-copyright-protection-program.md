## MakerWorld Launches Creator Copyright Protection Program
![image](https://blog.bambulab.com/content/images/2026/02/MW-01.png)

MakerWorld has introduced a new program aimed at protecting the intellectual property rights of 3D printing creators. This initiative is designed to help designers and artists safeguard their original works from unauthorized reproduction and distribution, ensuring that creators can maintain control over their designs in the rapidly evolving 3D printing landscape.
