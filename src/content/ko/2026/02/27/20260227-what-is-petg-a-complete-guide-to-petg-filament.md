## What Is PETG? A Complete Guide to PETG Filament
![image](https://cdn.creality.com/ow/official/57ca51d1-0405-4656-a924-3f0130e6ba82.png)

PETG (Polyethylene Terephthalate Glycol) is a popular 3D printing filament known for its durability, flexibility, and ease of use. It offers excellent layer adhesion and is resistant to impact and moisture, making it suitable for a variety of applications, including functional prototypes and end-use parts. The guide covers its properties, advantages, and best practices for printing with PETG.
