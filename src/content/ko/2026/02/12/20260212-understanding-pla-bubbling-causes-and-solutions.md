## Why Is My PLA Bubbling? Causes and Easy Fixes
![image](https://cdn.creality.com/ow/official/3c905ffa-57d5-4a7b-ae0b-64e3ac0cb596.png)

This article explores the common issue of bubbling in PLA filament during 3D printing. It identifies key causes such as moisture absorption, incorrect temperature settings, and poor filament quality. The article also provides practical solutions to mitigate bubbling, including proper storage of filament, adjusting printer settings, and using high-quality materials to ensure smoother prints.
