## Texas A&M Formula SAE Electric Team Accelerates Development with 3D Printing
![image](https://blog.bambulab.com/content/images/2026/02/Texas-AM-Formula-SAE-Electric-Team-14.jpg)

The Texas A&M Formula SAE Electric Team utilized 3D printing technology to enhance their vehicle development process, allowing for rapid prototyping and testing of components. This approach enabled the team to iterate designs quickly, reduce lead times, and improve overall performance in their electric race car.
