![image](https://cdn.creality.com/ow/official/192ae3d9-8578-4d95-95c9-f8fa8babd748.png)

This article discusses the safety considerations of using ABS and PLA filaments in 3D printing, focusing on the fumes emitted during printing and the associated health risks. It emphasizes the importance of proper ventilation and protective measures when working with ABS due to its potentially harmful emissions, while highlighting PLA as a safer alternative with fewer fumes. The article also provides guidance on best practices for 3D printing enthusiasts to ensure a safe printing environment.
