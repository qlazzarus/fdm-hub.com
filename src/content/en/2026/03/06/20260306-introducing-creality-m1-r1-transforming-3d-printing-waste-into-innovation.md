## Introducing Creality M1 & R1: Transforming 3D Printing Waste into Innovation
![image](https://cdn.creality.com/ow/official/8cbde2f5-cbae-4106-8f83-58c8b922e1b1.jpg)

Creality has launched the M1 and R1 3D printers, designed to utilize waste materials from 3D printing processes. These innovative machines aim to reduce environmental impact while enabling users to create new projects from leftover filament, promoting sustainability in the 3D printing community.
