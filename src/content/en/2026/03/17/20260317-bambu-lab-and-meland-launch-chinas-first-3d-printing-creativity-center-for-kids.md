## Bambu Lab and meland Launch China's First 3D Printing Creativity Center for Kids
![image](https://blog.bambulab.com/content/images/2026/03/BBLxmeland---hero.jpg)

Bambu Lab, in collaboration with meland, has inaugurated China's first 3D Printing Creativity Center aimed at inspiring and educating children in the field of 3D printing. The center provides hands-on experiences and workshops to foster creativity and innovation among young learners.
