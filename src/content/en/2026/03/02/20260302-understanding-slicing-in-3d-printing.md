## Understanding Slicing in 3D Printing
![image](https://cdn.creality.com/ow/official/92d86936-853b-48b0-ba6b-08581d027a28.png)

Slicing is a crucial step in 3D printing that involves converting a 3D model into a set of instructions for the printer. This process breaks down the model into layers, determining how each layer will be printed, including parameters like speed, temperature, and infill density. Proper slicing ensures accurate and efficient printing, impacting the final quality of the printed object.
