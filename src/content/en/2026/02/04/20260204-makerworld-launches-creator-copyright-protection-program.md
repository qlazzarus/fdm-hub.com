## MakerWorld Launches Creator Copyright Protection Program
![image](https://blog.bambulab.com/content/images/2026/02/MW-01.png)

MakerWorld has introduced a new program aimed at protecting the intellectual property rights of 3D printing creators. This initiative is designed to help artists and designers safeguard their original designs from unauthorized use and reproduction, fostering a more secure environment for innovation in the 3D printing community.
