## Understanding Vase Mode in 3D Printing: A Beginner's Guide
![image](https://cdn.creality.com/ow/official/0da065f6-dee9-4a82-b986-4f491426437e.png)

Vase mode is a 3D printing technique that allows for the creation of seamless, single-walled prints. This mode optimizes the printing process by reducing material usage and print time, making it ideal for producing decorative items and prototypes. The guide covers the benefits, settings adjustments, and tips for achieving the best results when using vase mode.
