## Bambu Lab Printers Aid Humpback Whale Conservation with Drones
![image](https://blog.bambulab.com/content/images/2026/01/Whaser-Project--4-.jpg)

The WHASER Project utilizes modern drones to enhance the conservation efforts for humpback whales, with significant support from Bambu Lab's 3D printers. These printers facilitate the rapid prototyping and production of drone components, enabling researchers to efficiently monitor whale populations and their habitats.
