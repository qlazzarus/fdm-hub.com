## How to Eliminate Layer Lines in 3D Prints: Causes and Solutions
![image](https://cdn.creality.com/ow/official/03f9f630-55b7-45d9-bbee-c30281a2d3b9.png)

This article explores the common causes of layer lines in 3D prints and offers practical solutions to achieve smoother finishes. Key factors include printer settings, material choice, and post-processing techniques. By adjusting parameters like layer height and print speed, as well as utilizing methods such as sanding and chemical smoothing, users can significantly improve print quality.
